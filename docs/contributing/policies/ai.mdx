# No ownership no innovation.

We believe AI is a general machine. It excels at the common patterns. However, when you work on the unknowns, AI struggles. AI struggled at understanding what Walker is, because the concept did not exist before.

We call contributors to respect and uphold the foundation principles of Walker. We built this project for those who care. If you want to explore the power of agent-maintained projects, feel free to fork & rebrand the project.

As for us, we still write code by hand because we believe that is the human pace of information consumption, that is how we own our work. We still write document by hand because we feels alive painting the future we believe in.

# When to use AI

The recommended tasks for AI are:

- Boring refactors
- Easy bugs
- Debugging
- Suggest solution
